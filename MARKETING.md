# Garda Handwriting Text OCR Marketing

## Positioning

Garda brings user-initiated handwriting transcription into an Obsidian vault and stores the result as searchable Markdown.

## Short description

Transcribe handwritten images and PDFs into Markdown, then copy the text, append it to a note, replace an embed, or create a transcription note.

## Product copy

Turn a selected handwritten image or PDF into Markdown text from Obsidian. Garda processes pages through a configured remote OCR service, shows progress and quality flags, and gives you control over where the transcription goes. Folder batches can process scans in sequence; the original attachment stays in the vault unless you explicitly replace its embed.

## Verified product points

- Image and PDF transcription from Obsidian file and editor actions.
- Clipboard, append, embed replacement, and new-note destinations.
- Sequential folder batches with progress and cancellation.
- Per-page quality and manual-review indicators.
- A local cache for unchanged files and Constance page-credit billing.
- A remote backend and OCR model are required; Garda is not an offline OCR tool.

## Claim boundaries

Do not promise perfect handwriting recognition, superiority over another OCR system, model-derived confidence scores, zero provider retention, or that remote OCR is private by default. Garda's backend forwards page images to its configured OCR provider. Users should review the provider's current terms before processing sensitive content.
